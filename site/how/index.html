Simple by design.

Hydra moves control out of the platform and into your hands.

  1. 1

    Pick a Topic

    Join an existing /h/ topic or start a new one. No one needs to grant permission or hand over ownership.

    Make a Persona

    Your persona belongs to you, not Hydra. Create more than one when different parts of your life should stay apart.

    Choose Your Flock

    Trust specific people for follows, blocks, silences, pins, or removals. Every choice can be different for every topic.

    Join In

Clear Boundaries

Hydra tells you what stays private and what becomes public.

Stays Local

  • Drafts
  • Private lists
  • Revisit entries
  • Credentials
  • Local moderation

Goes Public

  • Published posts
  • Published replies
  • Public profiles
  • Public reactions
  • Published judgments

Hydra publishes only when you take an action that clearly publishes.

Built to Survive

The app is replaceable. The community is not.

PeopleOpen protocolMany apps

Hydra uses Nostr, keeps readable local copies of public writing, and has no required Hydra server. Another compatible app can join the same conversation.
