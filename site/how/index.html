How Hydra works.

Hydra has the familiar parts of a forum, but no site owner at the center.

  1. 1

    Pick a topic

    Join an existing /h/ topic or start a new one. Topics have no owner and do not need to be created on a central server.

    /h/gardeningOwnerless topic
  2. 2

    Make a persona

    A persona is your identity in Hydra. You can make several and keep unrelated parts of your life separate.

    AMJ
  3. 3

    Choose your flock

    Copy the blocks, silences, pins, and removals of people you trust. Your choices can change from topic to topic.

    You←Mara’s pins
  4. 4

    Join in

    Post, reply, vote, send messages, save things, and discuss community norms. It still works like a forum.

    One discussionAcross matching topics

Private means private

Hydra draws a hard line between material on your computer and things you publish.

Stays Local

  • Drafts
  • Private lists
  • Revisit entries
  • Credentials
  • Local moderation

Goes Public

  • Published posts
  • Published replies
  • Public profiles
  • Public reactions
  • Published judgments

Nothing becomes public just because you typed it or looked at it. Publishing takes an explicit action.

The app is replaceable

The conversation does not have to disappear with it.

PeopleOpen protocolMany apps

Hydra speaks Nostr and keeps readable local copies of public writing. It does not depend on a Hydra server. A compatible app can take its place and keep talking to the same network.
