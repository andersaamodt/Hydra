No company can ban you from Hydra as a whole. People control their own view—not everyone else’s.

Ownerless Communities

Hydra gives you clear choices instead of one secret engagement machine.

  • Private RevisitSave something for yourself without making it a public signal.
  • Separate personasKeep different parts of your online life apart.
